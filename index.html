PDD Digital Hub for New Interns

A digital induction and development space for interns joining the Programme Design and Development Unit. Built on the GROW coaching model, this hub grows with you from your first day through every function you'll work in.

Welcome to the PDD Unit

This hub is your home base for the internship. Once you're unlocked into your own private space, you'll move through induction and the different functions of the unit, from admin and the LMS through to creative design and course development, all in one place. Everything you need to get oriented, reflect on your progress and check in with your coach lives inside your Intern Space.

Our theoretical basis: the GROW model

Your development through this internship is guided by the GROW coaching model. Each month, in your private space, you'll work through these four steps with your coach to reflect on how far you've come and what's next.

🎯 Goal

What do you want to achieve in this function, this month?

🔍 Reality

Where are you right now, honestly? What can you already do?

💡 Options

What support, people or resources could help you grow?

🚀 Way Forward

What will you actually do before your next check-in?

What's inside your Intern Space

🧭 InductionMeet the unit, the team and how PDD fits into CTLI and WCED.
📋 AdministrationLearn the admin functions that keep the unit running.

Head to your private Intern Space and pick which function you're busy with each month. The reference material, tasks and knowledge tasks for that function all live there, alongside your GROW planner, journal and progress dashboard.

About You
